**The Gilded Vault** — a top-down museum heist game. Three heists: steal the Star Sapphire from the Gallery, crack the Vault Floor, then grab the Crown Jewels on the Rooftop and sprint for the getaway van before the lockdown timer ends. Guards patrol with raycast-occluded vision cones, cameras sweep the halls, and tripwire laser grids toggle on and off — time your crossing. Sneak (Shift), sprint (Ctrl), grab loot with E, and spend loot points on smoke bombs, EMP pulses and lockpicks. Caught three times and the heist fails; never spotted and you keep the +500 ghost bonus. Zero dependencies, zero network, WebAudio synth sound only.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080/game/ in desktop Chrome.
